About NelumboMC

NelumboMC is a small multiplayer server running NightfallCraft: The Casket of Reveries — a Souls-like RPG experience for Minecraft featuring difficult combat, bosses, dungeons, exploration, quests, story progression, custom content, and approximately 197 mods.

This is not ordinary survival Minecraft. Every encounter can punish carelessness. Every dungeon holds something worth finding. Progression is earned through combat mastery, exploration, and story-driven quests — not idle grinding.

NelumboMC is new, with a small and friendly community. That is a strength: you can join from the very beginning, shape the world alongside others, and grow the community together rather than arriving late to an established hierarchy.

Features

Epic Fight Combat

Souls-like melee combat with dodging, blocking, and parrying. Every enemy demands respect — mistimed rolls and lazy swings will cost you dearly.

Bosses & Dungeons

Powerful bosses guard dangerous dungeons and custom structures. Prepare your gear, learn attack patterns, and conquer what lurks below.

Story & Quests

Story-driven quests with custom dialogue guide your progression. Follow the narrative, uncover secrets, and advance through a crafted RPG experience.

Exploration

Mysterious areas and dangerous locations fill the world. Venture beyond familiar biomes into the unknown — rewards favor the bold.

197 Mods

A curated modpack of approximately 197 mods transforms Minecraft into a full dark fantasy RPG — combat systems, progression, content, and atmosphere.

Small Community

A chill multiplayer experience with a friendly, growing player base. Join early, make connections, and help build something together from the ground up.

The World

Beyond the familiar blocks of Minecraft lies a realm reshaped by darkness. NightfallCraft transforms the overworld into a land of peril — where fog rolls through cursed valleys, ruins hold forgotten power, and every horizon promises both danger and discovery.

Dangerous locations test your resolve. Mysterious areas conceal lore and loot. Dungeons spiral deep beneath the surface, each chamber guarded by challenging enemies and culminating in encounters with powerful bosses. RPG progression ties your growth to exploration, quest completion, and combat mastery.

This is a world that does not hand you victory. It offers it — if you are willing to earn it.
